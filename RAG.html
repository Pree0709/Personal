Explore Retrieval-Augmented Generation, one step at a time. Change the source, tune the chunks, and watch a question find its evidence.

Two distinct phases
1–3: Prepare and index your knowledge.
4–6: Retrieve and answer for each question.

Learning simulation • No API key needed

Everything runs locally. Vectors use a simple word-based model; the final answer is an extractive simulation, not output from a real LLM. Nothing is uploaded or trained.
